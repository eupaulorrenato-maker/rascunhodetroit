## 💡 The New 2-Path Architecture

### 1. The Decision Screen (The Fork)
When the lead arrives, they are presented with a clear, welcoming choice:
* **Option 1: Quick In-Home Visit (30 Seconds)**
  * *"Don't feel like typing on a screen? Just leave your name, phone number, and address. We'll send a polite, licensed exterior specialist to inspect your home and give you a written estimate in person for free."*
* **Option 2: 2-Minute Online Diagnostic**
  * *"Answer 4 easy visual questions about your roof, siding, or deck. We’ll calculate whether you need a repair or full replacement, show you material options, and give you an estimated price range instantly."*

---

### 2. Path A: The Simple In-Home Booking Form
* **Designed for zero friction:**
  1. What to look at (Big buttons: Roofing, Siding, Decking, or Multiple)
  2. Full Name
  3. Phone Number (with clear reassurance: *"Only used to confirm your appointment"*)
  4. Street Address & Detroit Zip Code (auto-recognizes Detroit neighborhoods like Royal Oak, Birmingham, Boston-Edison)
  5. Best time to stop by (Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, Any Time)
  6. Optional notes
* Large green submission button: **"Confirm My Free In-Person Visit"**
* Reassuring confirmation screen with appointment recap and direct phone dispatch hotline.

---

### 3. Path B: The Re-engineered 4-Question Diagnostic
Streamlined to **only 4 clear, low-stress questions** with plain-English UX writing:
1. **Question 1:** Choose specialty (🏠 Roof, 🏡 Siding, 🪵 Deck)
2. **Question 2:** What seems to be the primary issue? (Plain language: active leaks, blown-off shingles, ice dams, wood rot, splintering wood, or just worn out)
3. **Question 3:** What type of outcome are you looking for? (Localized repair, full replacement with 50-year warranty, or advise me based on my answers)
4. **Question 4:** Choose your preferred material tier:
   * **Good Value:** Reliable, budget-friendly (Standard Architectural Shingles / Vinyl / Pressure Treated)
   * **Better Protection (Most Popular in Michigan):** Hail and freeze-resistant, lowers heating bills (Class 4 ClimateFlex / Insulated Vinyl / Trex Composite)
   * **Best Lifetime:** Permanent zero-maintenance (Metal Standing Seam / James Hardie / Capped PVC)

### 4. Instant Results & Transparent Price Range
* Shows an honest plain-English recommendation: *"We Recommend a Full System Replacement"* or *"A Targeted Repair is Likely Sufficient"*.
* 2–3 friendly bullet points explaining **why** (referencing Detroit winters and ice dams).
* Transparent price range (e.g. `$8,900 – $13,200`) with duration (`1 – 2 Days`) and yard magnet nail sweep guarantee.
* Simple 3-field form at the bottom to lock in the estimate and request a free on-site verification.
